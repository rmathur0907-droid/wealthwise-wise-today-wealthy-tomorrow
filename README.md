# WealthWise
**Wise today, Wealthy tomorrow**

##  Overview
Wealth Wise is an AI financial management platform that helps users understand, manage, and improve their financial decisions through AI-powered insights and interactive financial tools.
Designed with Indian users in mind, it supports INR (₹), UPI transaction records, SIPs, mutual funds, FDs, RDs, and other Indian financial concepts.

##  Problem Statement
Managing finances can be complicated, particularly for individuals with limited financial knowledge and small businesses without dedicated financial management resources.

Common challenges include:

- Difficulty tracking daily income and expenses.
- Lack of awareness about spending patterns.
- Poor monthly budgeting and saving habits.
- Limited understanding of investment options.
- Difficulty managing shared family expenses.
- Challenges in tracking small-business cash flow.
- Lack of accessible financial guidance.

**Our Solution:** Wealth Wise uses AI to convert financial information into understandable insights and practical recommendations.

##  Key Features

### 1. Personal Finance
- Track monthly income and expenses.
- Create personalised monthly budgets.
- Monitor savings and spending patterns.
- Track recurring payments.
- Set financial goals.
- Receive AI-powered spending insights.

### 2. Family Finance
- Create shared family finance groups.
- Invite family members.
- Track collective household expenses.
- Split expenses between members.
- Set individual spending limits.
- Create shared savings goals.
- Generate family financial reports.

### 3. Small Business Finance
- Track business income and expenses.
- Monitor operational costs.
- Manage customer payments.
- Calculate estimated profit and loss.
- Generate monthly financial reports.
- Forecast future cash flow.
- Monitor outstanding payments.
- Plan business loans and repayments.

### 4. WealthWise AI Assistant
An intelligent financial companion that helps users understand their finances through natural-language conversations.

Users can ask questions such as:
- How much did I spend this month?
- Where can I reduce my expenses?
- How much should I save every month?
- Explain SIPs in simple language.
- Analyse my investment portfolio.
- How much profit did my business make?
- How can I improve my cash flow?

The AI assistant uses available user-provided financial information to generate relevant insights rather than inventing financial data.

### 5. Smart Budgeting & Expense Tracking
- Manual income and expense entries.
- Transaction categorisation.
- UPI, cash, debit card, and credit card payment methods.
- Bank statement imports.
- CSV, Excel, and PDF transaction uploads.
- Category-wise spending analysis.
- Budget utilisation indicators.
- AI-powered expense recommendations.

### 6. Investment & Portfolio Management
Learn about and manage different investment options.

Supported investment concepts:
- Systematic Investment Plans (SIPs).
- Mutual Funds.
- Index Funds.
- Fixed Deposits (FDs).
- Recurring Deposits (RDs).
- Stocks.
- Compound Interest.

Includes interactive SIP, FD, RD, and compound interest calculators, along with portfolio tracking and educational AI insights.

### 7. Loan & EMI Calculator
- Calculate monthly loan EMIs.
- View total interest payable.
- Calculate total repayment amounts.
- Compare different loan amounts and durations.
- Understand principal versus interest distribution.

### 8. Multilingual Support
Designed to make financial management accessible to users across India.
Supported languages:
- English
- Hindi
- Kannada
- Tamil
- Telugu
- Malayalam
- Bengali
- Marathi

### 9. Savings Goals & Gamification
- Monthly savings challenges.
- Personal and family financial goals.
- Achievement badges.
- Spending reduction challenges.
- Visual savings progress tracking.

### 10. Guest Mode & User Accounts
- Explore the platform without registration.
- Save temporary information locally.
- Register using email or Google authentication.
- Synchronise financial information across devices.
- Secure database storage.
- Export financial reports.

## Security & Privacy

Financial privacy is a major consideration in Wealth Wise.

The platform is designed around:

- Secure user authentication.
- Supabase Row Level Security (RLS).
- User-specific financial records.
- Secure API key management.
- Permission-based document uploads.
- Financial data export and deletion.
- No collection of UPI PINs, banking passwords, OTPs, or card PINs.

Wealth Wise provides financial education and analytical insights. It does not guarantee investment returns or execute real-money investments.

## What Works

- **Unified Financial Management:** Wealth Wise brings Personal, Family, and Business Finance together in one platform.
- **Personal Finance:** Helps users track income, expenses, budgets, and savings.
- **Family Finance:** Provides features for managing shared household expenses and financial goals.
- **Small Business Finance:** Supports business income and expense tracking, profitability analysis, and cash-flow management.
- **AI Financial Assistant:** Provides conversational financial assistance and helps users understand their financial information.
- **Smart Budgeting:** Helps users monitor spending patterns and manage monthly budgets.
- **Investment Tools:** Provides investment education and financial calculators for SIPs, FDs, RDs, and compound interest.
- **Loan & EMI Calculator:** Helps users understand loan repayments, interest, and EMI breakdowns.
- **Interactive Dashboard:** Presents financial information through visual summaries, charts, and reports.
- **Indian Financial Context:** Designed around INR, UPI transactions, and commonly used Indian financial concepts.

*Note: Keep only features that have been tested and are functional in the deployed version.*

---

## What Does Not Work / Current Limitations

- **Automated Banking Integration:** Direct synchronisation with bank accounts and UPI applications requires authorised financial data integrations.
- **Transaction Import Accuracy:** Uploaded financial statements may require additional validation to improve transaction extraction and categorisation.
- **AI Accuracy:** AI-generated financial insights may require refinement to improve accuracy and contextual understanding.
- **Cash-Flow Forecasting:** Forecast accuracy depends on the availability and quality of historical financial records.
- **Multilingual Refinement:** Financial terminology and AI responses in regional languages require further testing.
- **Cross-Device Synchronisation:** Requires complete backend and database integration to ensure consistent financial records across devices.

*These are areas for further validation and development, not necessarily confirmed failures.*
